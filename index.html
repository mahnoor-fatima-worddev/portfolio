05 — faq

Frequently asked questions

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope — a business site usually takes 1–2 weeks, while a full WooCommerce store takes longer depending on how many products and features are involved. I'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes — I work remotely with clients worldwide through Upwork, WhatsApp, and email, and keep communication easy across time zones.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just your content (text, images, logo if you have one) and a rough idea of the pages or features you need. I'll guide you through the rest.

Do you offer support after the site is launched?

Yes — I offer post-launch fixes and updates, and can walk you through how to update your own site if you'd like to manage content yourself.

What platforms and tools do you build with?

WordPress with Elementor for design and layout, and WooCommerce for anything involving online stores and payments.
